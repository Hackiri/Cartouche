# 137. Cartouche installs, lists, renews and uninstalls apps of their own on this iPhone through its built-in SideStore

2026-11-13

Cartouche installs an IPA as an app of its own on this iPhone through its built-in SideStore, lists the apps it installed that way, renews them and uninstalls them, on its own screens: the System page's **Apps on this iPhone** ([`CartoucheIPhoneAppsView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheIPhoneAppsView.swift)), each app with its App ID as SideStore's App IDs screen lists it, when its signature expires, **Open**, **Renew** and **Uninstall**, and **Install an IPA on this iPhone** from Files; under them, **App IDs** lists the App IDs Apple keeps for the Apple ID, as SideStore's App IDs page does (name, bundle identifier, Apple's identifier, expiry, the one that expires first first), fetched on demand through a fourth action ([patch 0027](../../app/patches/sidestore/0027-list-the-app-ids-for-cartouche.patch)), so that an install refused for the ten App IDs a free Apple ID registers in seven days shows which ones hold them; **Import** on the home keeps installing inside Cartouche. SideStore gets three headless actions ([patch 0026](../../app/patches/sidestore/0026-install-renew-and-remove-apps-for-cartouche.patch)), which Cartouche runs in LiveProcess through LiveContainer's bridge ([`CartoucheApps.swift`](../../launcher/SideStoreSupport/CartoucheApps.swift)) as it runs *Refresh All Apps*, their request left in the app group (`CartoucheAppsRequest`); SideStore keeps the list of the apps Cartouche installed there (`CartoucheInstalledApps`), which *Refresh All Apps* and the background refresh renew with Cartouche ([patch 0017](../../app/patches/sidestore/0017-renew-cartouche-alone.patch), amending 64), so **Renew now** and the nightly automation keep them alive; a renewal that left some out says so under each, from the apps the outcome names ([`CartoucheIPhoneApps.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheIPhoneApps.swift), [`CartoucheIPhoneAppList.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheIPhoneAppList.swift)). Before anything goes to Apple, the install refuses, in Cartouche's words, an app the shared database holds that is not on the list, another store's, and an install that would pass the three active apps of a free Apple ID. **Uninstall** removes the app from the iPhone, or clears SideStore's record of one already gone from the home screen. With a p12 and its profile (78) the section says nothing can be installed. No page of SideStore's shows: 24 holds. Games never go through SideStore. The bundle identifiers of these apps never leave the iPhone.

**Why.** The player asked for what a standalone SideStore gives, from Cartouche's own screens, since Cartouche has SideStore built in and they will never install SideStore as an app of its own (2026-11-13). On the System page rather than the shelf: the shelf stays one shelf of things that open in Cartouche (135), and these apps belong with the signature and renewal they depend on. The list is the line 64 drew: the shared database also holds another store's apps, which the install leaves to that store and the renewals never touch. SideStore checks the three-app limit in My Apps alone and deactivates an app after the App ID is spent, hence the refusal first. Not tried on an iPhone yet.
